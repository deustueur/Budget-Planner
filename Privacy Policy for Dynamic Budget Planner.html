Privacy Policy for Dynamic Budget Planner Effective Date: 05/06/2026 1. Introduction Dynamic Budget Planner ("the App") is a personal financial tool designed to help users calculate and visualize their budgets. This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles your data. 2. Information Collection and Use The App requires access to your Google Sheets data to function. Specifically, it requests the "See, edit, create, and delete all your Google Sheets spreadsheets" permission. * The App uses this permission solely to read your financial inputs and generate dashboard metrics within the specific spreadsheet you are actively using. * The App does NOT store your data on any external servers. * The App does NOT share, sell, or distribute your data to any third parties. All processing happens locally within your Google account. 3. Data Security Because the App does not store your data externally, the security of your financial information relies entirely on the security of your Google account. We recommend using strong passwords and enabling two-factor authentication on your Google account. 4. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
